# Demand generator validation

Bundle `cambridge-2024`, model `day_types` (alpha 1024, beta 512) refitted on 195 training days; compared on 49 held-out days of 2024-04-01 – 2024-11-30 (seeded split, as in the model selection). Generated: `PoissonDemand`, 5 seeds per day. Reference: requests recorded on the held-out days. **Empirical** baseline: the recorded requests of the training days of the same group (reusing past data as-is).

Lower is better for every metric except the daily ratio (1 = same volume). TVD = total variation distance; JSD in bits; KS = Kolmogorov–Smirnov statistic of in-city ride times.
